Green Shell

My passion for architecture stems from the integration of organic morphology, technological innovation, and public welfare. This philosophy is materialized in "Green Shell," a parametric eco-pavilion designed for Toledo, Ohio. Developed using Rhino and Grasshopper, the project utilizes generative algorithms to construct a complex triangular shell structure. The continuous surface is populated with nodes and transformed into an intricate network of hollowed triangular panels.

Beyond formal exploration, this project is a conscious act of placemaking. By harmoniously blending art and nature, it offers an urban oasis where visitors can experience the dynamic interplay of light, shadow, and open space. Inspired by Georgia Tech architecture lecturer Marco Ancheita's belief that design must deeply interact with its site, "Green Shell" acts as a responsive organism. Its porous canopy filters sunlight to nourish the native greenery below, effectively generating a localized microclimate that breathes life into the urban fabric.
