01 // The Philosophy

Exquisite & Graceful.

Founded by Xin (Louis) He, Triumphal Architecture derives its philosophy from the precision of draft schemas and the enduring triumph of structure. Our mission is to seamlessly integrate architecture with our clients' visions, overturning traditional paradigms to enhance modern aesthetics.

As an incoming B.S. Architecture student at UIUC and a multi-award winner of the AIA Toledo HSDC, Louis He brings a rigorous, sincere attitude to every project, ensuring "High Quality" and "Safety" from physical constructions to virtual game environments.
