01 // The Philosophy

Exquisite & Graceful.

Founded by Xin (Louis) He, Triumphal Architecture derives its philosophy from the precision of draft schemas and the enduring triumph of structure. Our mission is to seamlessly integrate architecture with our clients' visions, overturning traditional paradigms to enhance modern aesthetics.

As an incoming B.S. Architecture student at UIUC and a multi-award winner of the AIA Toledo HSDC, Louis He brings a rigorous, sincere attitude to every project, ensuring "High Quality" and "Safety" from physical constructions to virtual game environments.

Parametric Design
Fine Art / Sculptures AP 3-D Art & Design

"I explored the relationship between material progress and ecological damage. If animals must evolve to survive human impact."

This conceptual matrix runs through my artworks in various sculptural subject matters, observing how humans relentlessly seek convenience, efficiency, technology, and personal interest without thought of ecological impact. My work serves as a stark advocate for protection and empathy toward vulnerable species.

Evolution
Dimensions: 8.25 x 8.25 x 4.5 in.
Materials: Ceramic, iron chain

The severely compromised environment forces the gastropod to become grotesquely personified, developing human-like teeth as a direct morphological consequence of anthropocentric impact.

Residuum
Dimensions: 1.5 x 10 x 10 in.
Materials: Ceramic, natural sand, scoop-net, acrylic, found object

Mimicking a degraded shoreline topography where the empty oyster shell completely loses its organic flesh, discarded helplessly amongst synthetic maritime refuse.

Decay
Dimensions: 2 x 10.5 x 8 in.
Materials: Clay, preserved moss, plastic, water bottle, warning sticker

A heavy infiltration of commercial plastic components illustrates the bio-chemical hazards leaking into the soil, creating a toxic contrast against vibrant, living green moss.

Toxification
Dimensions: 2.5 x 11.75 x 7 in.
Materials: Ceramic, foamcord, collage magazine print

Erupting pustules and scales map out fatal aquatic mutations, while deep lacerations and missing flesh signify invasive human scientific experimentation on weak lives.

Delicacy
Dimensions: 2.5 x 10 x 10 in.
Materials: Ceramic, acrylic paint, porcelain plate, salt, black pepper

An indictment on commodification and human desire. The severed bear paw remains a brutal, mutilated limb despite being masked by haute-cuisine culinary staging and seasoning.

Skin of Envy
Dimensions: 5.5 x 8 x 7.25 in.
Materials: Ceramic, plastic wrap, barcode

Plastic wrap and commercial barcodes tightly suffocate the reptile, directly symbolizing the ruthless commodification and consumption of snakeskin in luxury fashion.

Echoes of Wrath
Dimensions: 8 x 8.5 x 6.75 in.
Materials: Ceramic, synthetic plastic rings, acrylic paint

By forcibly seizing underwater territories, human naval systems deploy destructive sonar wave frequencies, leaving violent concentric scars embedded in the cephalopod's flesh.

Feeding Gluttony
Dimensions: 20 x 20 x 14 in.
Materials: Cardboard, natural straw, corn kernels, wooden dowel rods, plastic

A mechanical feeding tube directly penetrates the consumer chicken's entrails, framing the animal as a mere manufacturing asset engineered to grow rapidly for human consumption.

Trophy of Greed
Dimensions: 23 x 27 x 10 in.
Materials: Wooden dowel rods, acrylic paint, Blender 3D printed joints

A structural, wireframe stag head is brutally pierced by hunting arrows, reflecting humanity's insatiable, archaic pursuit of dominance and trophy status mounted on a wall.

Theatre of Pride
Dimensions: 8 x 10.25 x 6.5 in.
Materials: Ceramic, shaped plastic, acrylic paint

Capturing the exact moment a captive cetacean leaps through an artificial ring of flame—turning individual systematic exploitation into mass spectacle and entertainment.

Two Seconds of Gravity
Dimensions: 6.75 x 9.5 x 6 in.
Materials: Ceramic, Blender 3D printed tire matrix, acrylic paint

The heavy, synthetic tread of a car tire conveys a crushing sense of urban oppression. The trapped amphibian is pinned to the asphalt, utterly unable to jump.

Packaged Relic
Dimensions: 19.5 x 8.5 x 8.5 in.
Materials: Ceramic, foamcord, wooden rod, collage newspaper, fabric thread

Utilizing printed financial media to construct a poached elephant tusk, bound rigidly within three stark pillars to show the literal conversion of wildlife into commercial capital.

The Rusted Howl
Dimensions: 7.25 x 6 x 4.5 in.
Materials: Ceramic, industrial steel screws

An apex predator's head is discarded callously on a pile of urban stones, its evolutionary fangs completely replaced with cold, threaded industrial machinery screws.

Infrastructure of Silence
Dimensions: 21.25 x 19 x 17.5 in.
Materials: Natural branches, coconut fiber, wire filaments, fractured eggshells

High-voltage transmission infrastructure stands looming directly over a fragile bird's nest, exposing the constant, unseen electro-magnetic hazards encroaching upon local ecosystems.

Imprisoned Freedom
Dimensions: 9 x 16 x 23 in.
Materials: Wire framework, Blender 3D printed hand mass, acrylic

An eagle engineered to yearn for the expansive sky is tightly clutched and anchored by a massive human hand, serving as an ultimate emblem of forced subjugation.
