Aurora Shelter "Home"

A humanitarian architecture project addressing displaced populations and emergency response. "Home" is conceptualized not merely as a physical shell, but as a framework for restoring dignity, security, and communal cohesion.

The design integrates rapid-assembly tectonic components with localized sustainable materials to form resilient, modular micro-communities, bridging the immediate need for emergency refuge with the long-term goal of social integration.
